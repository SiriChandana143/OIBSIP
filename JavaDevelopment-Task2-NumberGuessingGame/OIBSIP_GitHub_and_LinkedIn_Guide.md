✨ Key Technical Features:
🔹 Core Gameplay & Multi-Difficulty Rules: Easy (1-50), Medium (1-100), and Hard (1-200) with dynamic attempt progress indicators.

🔹 Player Session Analytics: Created a real-time analytics modal tracking total rounds, win rate %, average attempts, and overall score.
🔹 Procedural Audio Synthesis: Built an in-memory sound generator using `javax.sound.sampled` to synthesize custom frequency chimes for guess feedback—requiring zero external audio asset files!
